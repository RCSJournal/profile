Publication Ethics

RCS expects authors, reviewers and editors to uphold originality, transparency, confidentiality and research integrity.

Core principles

Manuscripts must be original and must acknowledge the work of others. Simultaneous submission to more than one journal is not permitted. Authorship should reflect genuine scholarly contribution. Relevant financial, institutional or personal conflicts must be disclosed. Reviewers must keep unpublished manuscripts confidential. Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism and unjustified manipulation of data or references are unacceptable. Credible concerns may lead to investigation and appropriate editorial action.

The journal's ethics framework is informed by recognized international publication-ethics principles, including COPE guidance.
