Data & Reproducibility

Authors should describe research data, materials, instruments or procedures sufficiently to allow readers to understand and evaluate the work. When appropriate and legally and ethically possible, underlying data or supplementary materials should be made available through a reliable repository or upon reasonable request.

Data and Research Materials

Authors should describe research data, materials, instruments or procedures sufficiently to allow readers to understand and evaluate the work. When appropriate and legally and ethically possible, underlying data or supplementary materials should be made available through a reliable repository or upon reasonable request.

Authors must not disclose confidential, personal or protected data without appropriate authorization.
