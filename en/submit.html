The page structure is active. Journal-specific information will be added later.

Until a dedicated online submission workflow is finalized, authors should contact the editorial office at mm.rubin@ut.ac.ir and provide a manuscript prepared according to the Author Guidelines. The manuscript must fit the journal's scope, be original, not be under consideration elsewhere, and have the approval of all listed authors.

Authors should also provide any required declarations concerning funding, conflicts of interest, research ethics, data and AI use.
