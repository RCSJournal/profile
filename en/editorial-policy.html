Editorial Policies

RCS editorial policy is based on scholarly quality, transparency, confidentiality, academic independence and research integrity.

Editorial principles

Submissions are first assessed for scope, completeness and basic scholarly requirements. Suitable manuscripts proceed to specialist peer review. Editorial decisions should be based on scholarly considerations and not on nationality, institutional affiliation, gender, language or personal relationships. Authors, editors and reviewers should disclose relevant conflicts of interest. Published articles may be corrected, accompanied by an expression of concern, or retracted when significant errors or integrity concerns require such action.

The journal seeks to maintain a transparent and consistent editorial process while protecting the confidentiality of submitted manuscripts.
