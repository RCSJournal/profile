About the Journal

Russian and Caucasus Studies is a biannual academic journal devoted to research on the Russian language, language education, linguistics, literature, culture, translation, and interdisciplinary studies related to Russia and the Caucasus.

Mission and scope

RCS provides a scholarly forum for original theoretical, empirical, comparative and applied research. The journal aims to strengthen academic dialogue among researchers, teachers, translators and students. Publication languages are Persian, Russian and English.
