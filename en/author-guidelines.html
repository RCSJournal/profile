Author Guidelines

Authors should submit original work that fits the scope of Russian and Caucasus Studies and follows the journal's ethical and scholarly requirements.

Before Submission

A manuscript should normally present a clear title, abstract, keywords, research objectives, appropriate methodology, analysis or results, conclusion and references. Articles may be submitted in Persian, Russian or English. All authors and affiliations must be listed accurately. Sources must be cited consistently and completely. Where applicable, authors should disclose funding, conflicts of interest, research-ethics information, data availability, author contributions and the use of AI tools.

Submission questions may be sent to mm.rubin@ut.ac.ir.
