Plagiarism

RCS does not accept plagiarism, unattributed copying, fabricated references or substantial reuse of previously published text presented as original. Authors must cite sources accurately and identify quotations and adapted material. Suspected cases may be investigated and may result in rejection, correction or retraction depending on the circumstances.
