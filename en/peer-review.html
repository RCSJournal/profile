Peer Review

Manuscripts that pass the initial editorial check are evaluated by qualified reviewers with relevant subject expertise.

Review Process

The process normally includes an initial editorial check, specialist peer review, revision where necessary, and a final editorial decision. Reviewers assess originality, research objectives, methodology, evidence, analysis, references, clarity and scholarly contribution. Reviewers must keep manuscripts confidential and disclose conflicts of interest. Revision does not guarantee acceptance, and the journal does not promise a fixed decision time.

Possible decisions include acceptance, revision or rejection.
