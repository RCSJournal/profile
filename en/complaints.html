Complaints and Appeals

Authors, reviewers and readers may raise concerns about editorial procedures, peer review, publication ethics or published content by contacting the editorial office at mm.rubin@ut.ac.ir. Complaints should provide enough information and evidence to allow the matter to be assessed.

The journal will review complaints fairly and confidentially and will seek to respond within a reasonable period.
