# Russian and Caucasus Studies (RCS)

**Russian and Caucasus Studies (RCS)** is an academic journal dedicated to scholarly research on the Russian language, literature, culture, linguistics, translation, and interdisciplinary studies related to Russia and the Caucasus.

## Official Website

The official journal website is:

**https://rlsj.ir/**

This GitHub organization provides an independent academic and technical identity for the journal, separate from any individual researcher or personal GitHub account.

## Scope

RCS welcomes original scholarly research in areas including:

- Russian language and linguistics
- Russian as a foreign language
- Russian literature and literary studies
- Translation studies
- Russian culture and civilization
- Comparative linguistic and literary studies
- Cultural studies
- Russia and the Caucasus
- Interdisciplinary approaches to Russian and Caucasus studies

## Publication Languages

The journal accepts scholarly articles in:

**Persian · Russian · English**

## Academic Collaboration

RCS is open to scholarly cooperation with researchers, universities, journals, research centers, and academic organizations working in Russian studies, Caucasus studies, and related fields.

## Repository Policy

This organization is used for the journal's website, technical resources, publication materials, and other appropriate open scholarly resources.

Confidential manuscripts, reviewer reports, personal data, and other non-public editorial materials should not be stored in public repositories.
